Open **Support** from the sidebar. It lists your requests to the StackBlaze team, with their replies: "New replies show up here as soon as they arrive." The filters show **All**, **Open**, **Pending** and **Solved** requests.

<Screenshot id="support/1-list" alt="Support: New request, the All, Open, Pending and Solved filters, and No requests yet" />

## Open a request

Click **New request**. **New support request** asks for:

- **Request type**: **Billing & payments**, **Account & access**, **Technical — app or deployment**, **General question** or **Other**;
- **Subject**, a short summary;
- **Description**: "Include timestamps and error text when relevant — it helps us respond faster."

<Screenshot id="support/2-new" alt="New support request: Request type, Subject and Description, with Cancel and Submit request" />

Click **Submit request**. The team replies on the request and by email.

Which support you get, and how fast, depends on your support plan; see [Support plans](/billing#support-plans).
