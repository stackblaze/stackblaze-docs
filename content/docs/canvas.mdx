Each environment of a project opens on its canvas: every app, database and add-on in that environment, with its status. The canvas is also where changes wait until you deploy them.

## Apps and Designer

**Apps** shows a card per service. Databases and caches are listed under **Databases**.

<Screenshot id="canvas/1-apps" alt="Apps view: the web card, and PostgreSQL and Valkey under Databases" />

Each card shows the service's name, its address and its status (**Running**, **Ready to deploy**, **Staged**, …). On an app card, the number with an arrow opens the databases the app is connected to, with their disk sizes.

<Screenshot id="canvas/6-instances" alt="The web card opened: connected to db (1Gi) and cache (1Gi)" />

**Designer** draws the same services as a diagram, with lines from each app to the databases it uses. Services are kept together in groups. The buttons at the bottom left are **Tidy up** (re-run the automatic layout), **Zoom in** and **Zoom out**.

<Screenshot id="canvas/2-designer" alt="Designer: the WEB group with web, Valkey and PostgreSQL, linked" />

A group's **…** menu has **Rename**, **Hide contents**, **Colour**, **Remove group**, **Save as stack…** and **Delete stack…**.

<Screenshot id="canvas/3-group-menu" alt="A group's menu: Rename, Hide contents, Colour, Remove group, Save as stack…, Delete stack…" />

### Groups

Cards sit in groups. Here the **NGINX** group holds the nginx service and its Email (SMTP) add-on, and PostgreSQL, Memcached, Kafka and the scheduled tasks each have a group of their own.

**Hide contents** in a group's **…** menu folds it into one card that counts what's inside, here "1 service · 1 add-on · 3 connections", with **contents hidden**. Its lines stay, drawn from the folded card. **Show contents** in the same menu opens it again.

<Screenshot id="canvas/d-3-collapsed" alt="The NGINX group folded: one card reading 1 service · 1 add-on · 3 connections, contents hidden" />

### Lines

A line joins a service to each database and add-on it's connected to. A line from a Kafka add-on is labelled with what it does, such as **publish**.

### Card menus

Right-click a card for its menu. The first line names the card and its kind. A service's menu has **Open app**, **Settings**, **Logs**, **Metrics**, **Files**, **Terminal**, **Rename**, **Backups**, **Develop**, **Restart**, **Pause**, **Delete** and **Group**, which opens a submenu.

<Screenshot id="canvas/d-menu-1" alt="The nginx service's right-click menu: Open app, Settings, Logs, Metrics, Files, Terminal, Rename, Backups, Develop, Restart, Pause, Delete, Group" />

Other cards have fewer items:

| Card | Menu |
| --- | --- |
| PostgreSQL | **Open SQL console**, **Open dashboard**, **Settings**, **Logs**, **Metrics**, **Rename**, **Backups**, **Group**, **Delete** |
| Memcached, Kafka | **Open dashboard**, **Settings**, **Logs**, **Metrics**, **Rename**, **Group**, **Delete** |
| Email (SMTP), Scheduled tasks | **Open dashboard**, **Rename**, **Group**, **Delete** |
| A volume | **Browse files**, **Rename**, **Delete** |

### New cards before they deploy

Something you add with **+ Service** first appears as a dashed card marked **ready to deploy**: it's on the canvas only, and nothing runs yet. Its right-click menu has **Deploy** and **Remove from canvas**. **Remove from canvas** drops it, and nothing was created.

<Screenshot id="canvas/d-4-ghost-menu" alt="A new nginx-2 card, ready to deploy, with its menu: Deploy and Remove from canvas" />

### Tidy up

**Tidy up** lays the groups out again in three columns, left to right: apps that only call other services, then services that other apps call, then AI models and what serves them. The columns come from the lines, not from names. Tall columns wrap into a new column.

### Card status

A card's last line shows its status, for example **Running**, **Updating** (a new version is rolling out), **Ready** for an add-on that has nothing to run, **ready to deploy** for a new card, or **Staged** for a change waiting to be committed.

The disk icon on a database card shows its volume. A database's disk is "managed by the database": to look at its data, use the database tools (see [PostgreSQL](/data/databases/postgresql)).

<Screenshot id="canvas/4-volume" alt="A database's volume: This disk is managed by the database" />

**Search this environment…** finds a service by name. Pick a result, or use the arrow keys and **Enter**, to open it.

<Screenshot id="canvas/5-search" alt="Searching for cache: Valkey under On this environment" />

## Add to the environment

**+ Service**, at the top right, opens **Add to** *environment*. What each item adds:

| Item | Adds | See |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Template** | one-click apps from the catalog | [Templates](/build-deploy/templates) |
| **GitHub repository** | an app built from a repository | [Deploy from GitHub](/build-deploy/deploy-from-github) |
| **Docker image** | an app from a public or private image | [Deploy a Docker image](/build-deploy/deploy-docker-image) |
| **Database or cache** | PostgreSQL, MariaDB, Valkey and more | [Databases](/data/databases/postgresql) |
| **Developer sandbox** | an isolated VM with a shell and checkpoints | [Developer sandboxes](/build-deploy/developer-sandbox) |
| **AI model (GPU)** | an open model on a GPU | [AI models](/ai/models) |
| **AI agent** | a chatbot or support agent with your instructions | [AI agents](/ai/agents) |
| **Function** | one TypeScript file, no build | [Functions](/build-deploy/functions) |
| **Add-on** | email, scheduled tasks, Memcached, ProxySQL | [Add-ons](/addons/email) |
| **Import** | services from a Compose file, render.yaml, fly.toml or stackblaze.yaml | [Import](/build-deploy/import) |
| **Backups** | snapshots and restore for the environment, once it has something in it | [Environment backups](/data/environment-backups) |

## Staged changes

Most changes you make to a running service, such as a new variable, are not applied straight away. They are staged, and the toolbar shows **Commit N change(s)**.

<Screenshot id="canvas/7-staged" alt="The canvas with Commit 1 change in the toolbar" />

Click it to see what will be applied to the environment: here **Configuration · web**, one setting. Then:

- **Deploy N change(s)** applies them. "Anything that fails stays staged."
- **Commit message** (optional) is shown in the service's **Deployment** history.
- **Discard all** drops every staged change, with no confirmation. Nothing is applied.

<Screenshot id="canvas/8-commit" alt="1 change to apply: Configuration · web, 1 setting; Commit message; Discard all; Deploy 1 change" />

<Screenshot id="canvas/11-message" alt="The commit dialog with the message Add CANVAS_DEMO" />

After **Deploy**, the service's **Deployment** tab shows the rollout with your message.
