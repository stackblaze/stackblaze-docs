Your organization's plan sets its included usage and limits. You change it on [Billing](/billing), under **Administration** in the sidebar. Upgrades apply right away; downgrades and cancellations at the end of the billing period.

## Current plan

The **Current plan** card shows the plan's name and price, for example **Business** **$499/mo**, and when the included usage starts again: "Included usage resets on Dec 11 (17 days left)".

From here you can open **Change plan** or **Cancel subscription**. On the Free Trial the button reads **Upgrade**. On the Free plan without a card it reads **Add a card** (see [The Free plan](#the-free-plan)).

While a downgrade is scheduled, the card says so, for example "Downgrading to Pro on December 1, 2026.", with **Cancel** next to it (see [Cancel a scheduled downgrade](#cancel-a-scheduled-downgrade)). After you cancel the subscription, it says "Your subscription ends on" the date and "You keep" the plan "until then.", with **Keep my plan**.

## Change plan

Click **Change plan**. The window opens with "Compare plans and change anytime. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades at period end." It shows one plan at a time: its name, what it's for, a price picker, **Monthly** and **Annual**, and **What's included:**. On the right it says what changing to that plan does.

<Screenshot id="billing/2-change-plan" alt="Change plan: Pro, $29 / month, Monthly and Annual, What's included, and Downgrade your plan on the right" />

Open the picker to see the plans you can switch to, listed under **Cloud** ("Organizations and shared hosting"). Your current plan isn't listed. Each row shows the price and what the plan is for.

<Screenshot id="billing/3-plan-list" alt="The plan picker open under Cloud: $10 / month Personal projects & MVPs and $29 / month Production apps & startups" />

Click **Annual** to see the yearly price. It's billed once a year, up front, and saves 20%: the picker reads, for example, **$278.40 / year** and "$23.20/mo billed annually · save 20%".

## Upgrade

Pick a higher plan in the picker. The right side reads **Upgrade your plan** and lists what happens:

- "Your new limits apply right away. Nothing that is running is restarted."
- "You'll be charged a prorated amount today for the remainder of this billing period."

The card you pay with is shown, "Charged for this upgrade". To pay with another card, click **Change** and enter the new card; **Use saved card instead** goes back to the saved one.

Click **Confirm upgrade for** the price, for example **Confirm upgrade for $29/month**. If your bank asks you to confirm the payment, it opens its confirmation step; the upgrade completes once you approve it. If the payment can't go through, the window shows why and nothing changes.

When it's done, the window shows **Plan updated** and "You're on" the new plan, with **Plan active now**. If your services were paused, it adds **Your apps are starting**: "Anything that was paused is coming back online." Click **Done** to close it.

### From the Free Trial

On the Free Trial, click **Upgrade**. The window reads "Choose a paid plan to unlock more templates and deploy heavier apps." There is no saved card yet, so you enter one in the window, then click **Subscribe for** the price, for example **Subscribe for $10/month**, or **Subscribe for $96/year** on **Annual**. Choosing a plan brings stopped services back.

### When a feature needs a higher plan

Some screens open the same window when what you tried needs a higher plan, for example "Upgrade your plan to unlock this.", "Pick a plan to bring your apps back online." or "Want to deploy Ghost? Upgrade your plan to unlock this template." The window then picks "the lowest plan that includes this" for you (**Selected for you**). You can still pick another plan.

## The plans

These are the self-serve plans and what **What's included:** lists for each.

| | Developer | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| For | Personal projects & MVPs | Production apps & startups | Growing teams & agencies |
| Monthly | $10 / month | $29 / month | $499 / month |
| Annual | $96 / year ($8/mo) | $278.40 / year ($23.20/mo) | $4,790.40 / year ($399.20/mo) |
| Included usage | $10/month | $29/month | $499/month |
| Projects | 5 | 25 | 100 |
| Services per project | 20 | 50 | 200 |
| Databases (Postgres, MariaDB, Redis) | 2 | 10 | 30 |
| Backups kept | 7 days | 14 days | 30 days |
| Size per service | Up to 2 vCPU · 4 GB RAM | Up to 4 vCPU · 16 GB RAM | Up to 8 vCPU · 32 GB RAM |
| Instances per service | 1 | Up to 3 | Up to 10 |
| Environments per project | 2 | 5 | 10 |
| Team members | 1 | 5 | 20 |
| Regions | 1 | 3 | 3 |

Every plan includes custom domains with automatic SSL and unlimited free build minutes. The plan price comes back as included usage: what your apps use is counted against it first (see [Usage](/billing/usage#usage-breakdown)).

Every plan, the Free plan and the Free Trial included, can add any add-on and deploy any template the platform offers. Plans differ only in the limits on this page. A template that needs more than your plan allows, such as more services or databases, or a bigger service than **Size per service**, is refused before anything is created.

The last three rows are the limits the [Plan limits](/billing/usage#plan-limits) card on Usage counts against.

**Size per service** is the most one service can use in total. A service with more than one instance splits it: each instance gets an equal share. A helper container that runs inside an instance, such as the private-network proxy, takes its part from that instance's share, and the app container always keeps at least 60% of it. Each run of a scheduled task gets the size of one instance, and runs of the same task never overlap.

| Connections per database | 100 | 100 | 200 | 400 | 500 |
| Connections per Valkey database | 50 | 50 | 200 | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| Database backups | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Highly available databases | No | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Preview environments | No | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| AI agents | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |

| Single sign-on, tracing, security scans, advanced networking | No | No | No | No | Yes |

A build over the limit isn't queued: it's refused, and you can start it again once a build finishes (see [Deploy from CI](/cli/ci#build-limits)).

## The Free Trial

A new account's first organization can start on the **Free trial**: 30 days or $5 of usage, whichever comes first, with no credit card. The trial is once per account; another organization needs a paid plan from the start.

During the trial, the organization has:

| | Free Trial |
| --- | --- |
| Size per service | Up to 0.5 vCPU · 1 GB RAM |
| Projects | 1 |
| Services per project | 3 |
| Environments per project | 1 |
| Instances per service | 1 |
| Databases | 2, each with a fixed 1 GiB of storage |
| Valkey memory | 25 MiB |
| Volumes | 1, up to 5 GB |
| Emails | 100 a day |
| Outbound data included | 5 GB |
| Team members | 1 |
| Regions | 1 |

The **Current plan** card shows **Free trial** and what happens when it ends, for example "On Dec 26 your workspace moves to the Free plan: $1 of usage every month, no card needed. Databases need a card on file.", with **Upgrade**.

When the 30 days are over or the $5 is used up, the organization moves to the [Free plan](#the-free-plan) by itself. Your apps keep running and nothing is deleted, except that [databases need a card](#databases-need-a-card). You get an email that says so. You can also choose a paid plan at any time with **Upgrade**.

## The Free plan

The **Free** plan has no monthly fee and no end date. It is where an organization lands after the Free Trial, and it is meant for side projects. What it includes depends on whether there is a card on file:

| | Free, no card | Free, card on file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | $0 |
| Included usage | $1 every month | $5 every month |
| When the included usage is used up | Your apps pause until the next month. Nothing is charged | Your apps keep running. Only the usage above $5 is charged |
| Databases | None: a database needs a card | 2, each with a fixed 1 GiB of storage |
| Volumes | 1, up to 5 GB | 1, up to 5 GB |
| Size per service | Up to 0.5 vCPU · 1 GB RAM | The same |
| Projects | 1 | 1 |
| Services per project | 3 | 3 |
| Environments per project | 1 | 1 |
| Instances per service | 1 | 1 |
| Valkey memory | n/a | 25 MiB |
| Emails | 100 a day | 100 a day |
| Team members | 1 | 1 |
| Regions | 1 | 1 |

### Without a card

- **$1 of usage every month, at no charge.** Your usage is counted against it first, at the standard rates (see [Usage](/billing/usage#usage-breakdown)).
- **When the $1 is used up, your apps pause.** They start again by themselves at the start of the next month. Nothing is ever charged, because there is no card to charge.
- **Your apps and volumes are kept** while they are paused.

The **Current plan** card shows **Free** and reads "$1 of usage every month, no card. Your apps pause when it is used and start again next month. Add a card for $5 every month and to run databases." The button is **Add a card**.

### Databases need a card

A database can't run on Free without a card. This covers every database kind, Key Value included.

| When | What happens to a database |
| --- | --- |
| During the Free Trial | It runs |
| The trial ends and there is no card | It is stopped. Its data is kept for 30 days. No new database can be created |
| You add a card within the 30 days | It starts again with its data |
| 30 days pass without a card | It is deleted with its data. It can't be restored |

You get an email when a database is stopped, another one 3 days before it is deleted, one when it is deleted, and one when it starts again after you add a card. The email that tells you the trial has ended says it too.

Creating a database without a card is refused with "A database needs a credit card on file. Add a card or choose a paid plan to create one."

PostgreSQL and MariaDB are stopped as soon as the trial ends. The other database kinds keep running until the 30 days are over and are then deleted the same way, so add a card before that date to keep them.

### With a card on file

- **Up to $5 of usage in a month: nothing.** Your usage is counted against the included $5 first, the same way a paid plan's included usage works (see [Usage](/billing/usage#usage-breakdown)).
- **Above $5: only the difference.** Your services keep running, and only the usage above $5 is charged to your card, at the standard rates. If you use $7.40 in a month, you pay $2.40.
- **Every month.** The $5 starts again with each monthly period. What you don't use doesn't carry over.

You get an email the first time you go past the included $5 in a month, before anything is invoiced.

To stop at $5 instead of paying for more, set **Spend limit** on [Usage](/billing/usage) to **Off**: your apps are then paused once the included usage is used up, until the next period. **Fixed** sets the most you can be charged in a month.

### Add a card

On Free without a card, click **Add a card** on the **Current plan** card. The [Change plan](#change-plan) window opens on **Free** with the description "Card on file: $5 of usage every month"; the paid plans are listed with it. Instead of a payment, the window asks for a card and says what it is for: "Free includes $5 of usage every month, at no charge." and "If your workspace uses more, it keeps running and only the usage above $5 is charged to this card, at the standard rates." Nothing is charged when you add it.

One card can hold one Free organization, and one account can own one. A second one is refused with "This card is already used for another workspace on the Free plan. Use a different card, or choose a paid plan." or "You already have a workspace on the Free plan. Choose a paid plan for this one."

Moving from a paid plan to Free is a [downgrade](#downgrade): it takes effect at the end of the period you paid for.

## Downgrade

Picking a lower plan shows **Downgrade your plan**: "Switch to Pro at the end of your billing period." The window lists:

- "Takes effect on" the date, and "You'll keep full access to" your current plan "until then."
- "No refund for the current billing period."
- "On" that date, "the new plan applies to your apps and databases right away."
- Any database that restarts for the new limits, for example "Database pg restarts once on December 1, 2026."

The button reads **Schedule downgrade**. Nothing changes until the date shown.

Before a downgrade, the window checks what you run against the lower plan and lists what changes:

- **Reduced at the end of your billing period**: for example, a smaller maximum size per service (larger services are resized and restarted), fewer database connections, or Redis connection limits. Databases restart with the new limits.
- **Over the new plan: keeps running, but you can't add more**: for example, "Your workspace has 11 databases; Pro includes 10."
- **Not included in the new plan**: for example, backups kept for fewer days. Existing schedules keep their retention until you save them again.

To leave without changing anything, close the window with **×**.

### Cancel a scheduled downgrade

Until the date, the **Current plan** card shows "Downgrading to" the plan "on" the date. Click **Cancel** next to it to keep your current plan. Nothing is charged or changed.

## Cancel subscription

Click **Cancel subscription** under **Change plan** on [Billing](/billing). The confirmation explains what happens.

<Screenshot id="billing/5-cancel" alt="Cancel your subscription? with Keep my plan and Cancel subscription" />

You keep your plan and everything running until the end of the period you already paid for, and you aren't charged again. After that your services are paused. Their apps and data are kept for 30 days, then deleted unless you choose a plan. Your account stays.

Click **Keep my plan** to close it without cancelling. After you cancel, **Keep my plan** stays on the **Current plan** card until the end date, so you can undo it.
