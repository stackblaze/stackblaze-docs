Once an [AI agent](/ai/agents) is deployed, you work with it from its card on the canvas and from its panel. This page goes through each of them. We tried every change on a real agent, on Qwen3 4B, and show what the agent did.

## Its card and menu

The agent's card shows its name, **service**, **open chat** and its status. **open chat** opens the [team chat page](#chat-with-it).

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/p-card" alt="The agent's card: bean, service, open chat, Running, next to its PostgreSQL" />

Right-click the card for its menu: **Open chat**, **Settings**, **Logs**, **Metrics**, **Files**, **Terminal**, **Rename**, **Backups**, **Develop**, **Restart**, **Pause**, **Delete** and **Group**. Most of them work as for any service. The ones that matter most for an agent are [below](#rename-it).

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/p-menu" alt="The card's menu: Open chat, Settings, Logs, Metrics, Files, Terminal, Rename, Backups, Develop, Restart, Pause, Delete, Group" />

## Its panel

Click the card to open its panel. The top shows the agent's name, its address, the agent image and the region. The address is for the [API](/ai/agent-channels#call-it-from-your-code): in a browser, use **open chat** instead.

The tabs are **Deployment**, **Agent**, **Variables**, **Logs**, **Metrics**, **Terminal** and **Settings**.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/p-tab-agent" alt="The agent's panel on Agent → General: Instructions, Model (Qwen/Qwen3-4B (AI Gateway)) and Tools, with Discard and Stage changes" />

**Agent** has a row of sections:

| Section | What's in it | How changes apply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **General** | **Instructions**, **Model** and **Tools** | Staged, then deployed |
| **Knowledge** | Files it can read | Saved at once: see [Knowledge](/ai/agent-knowledge-and-skills#knowledge) |
| **Skills** | Step-by-step notes for tasks | Saved at once: see [Skills](/ai/agent-knowledge-and-skills#skills) |
| **Integrations** | Outside services as tools | Saved at once, then the agent restarts: see [Integrations](/ai/agent-knowledge-and-skills#integrations) |
| **Triggers** | **Time**, **Webhook**, **API** and **Widget** | Schedules and the widget are staged. Keys and webhooks are saved at once. See [Reach your agent](/ai/agent-channels) |
| **Evals** | Repeatable tests | Saved at once: see [Test your agent](/ai/agent-evals) |
| **Optimizations** | Cheaper ways to run it | See [Optimizations](/ai/agent-evals#optimizations) |
| **Sessions** | Every chat, from every channel | Read only: see [below](#read-its-sessions) |

Each section's title has an **(i)** that explains it.

The other tabs are the usual service tabs:

- **Deployment**: each change you deploy is a new release, listed here with **Manual Deploy**.
- **Variables**: the agent's settings as variables. Those marked **Platform-managed**, like its model's address and key, are set by the platform. Change the agent in **Agent**, not here.
- **Logs**: one line each time it starts, and one line per chat turn. A turn line has the channel, how long it took (`ms`), the tokens in and out, how many tools it used and any error.
- **Metrics**, **Terminal** and **Settings**: as for any service. **Settings** has its visibility, domains, scale, sleep mode, volumes and the rest.

## Change its instructions

Changes in **General** are staged, like other canvas changes, and the agent restarts with them.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Edit and stage

Open **Agent** → **General** and change **Instructions**. A dot next to **General** shows there's an unsaved change. **Discard** puts the field back.

We replaced the empty instructions of our **Blank** agent with: *You are Bean, the assistant of Night Owl Coffee. Answer in at most two short sentences. End every answer with: Happy brewing!*

Click **Stage changes**. The toast says **Agent settings staged** and **Commit the changes to release them.** The section shows **staged: instructions**.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/st-ins-staged" alt="staged: instructions, the new instructions, and the toast Agent settings staged" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Deploy

Close the panel and click **Commit 1 change** on the canvas. The dialog lists the agent with **settings** and **changes instructions; a new release**. Click **Deploy 1 change**.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/st-ins-commit" alt="1 change to apply: ai-agent-5m73 settings, changes instructions; a new release, and Deploy 1 change" />

The agent restarts with the change. Ours was back to **Running** after about a minute and a half.

</Step>

<Step>

### Check the effect

Before the change, asked **Hi! In one sentence, who are you?**, the agent said **I am an AI assistant designed to help with tasks and answer questions.**

After it, asked **Hi! Who are you?**, it said **I am Bean, the assistant of Night Owl Coffee. I'm here to help you with any questions or needs you may have. Happy brewing!**

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/chat-ins-answer" alt="The chat page: Hi! Who are you? and the answer as Bean, ending with Happy brewing!" />

</Step>

</Steps>

## Turn its tools off

**Tools** in **General** works the same way: change it, **Stage changes**, commit and deploy. The commit dialog says **changes tools**.

We uploaded a small FAQ to **Knowledge**. It says the Night Owl blend is decaf. Then we asked twice: **Read faq.md in your knowledge folder, then tell me: do you sell decaf?**

- With **read**, **grep** and **glob** on, the agent used **read** and answered **Yes, we do sell decaf. The Night Owl blend is Swiss Water decaf. Happy brewing!**
- With every tool off (**No tools: answers from instructions only**), it couldn't open the file. It answered **Yes, we do sell decaf. It's a great choice for those who want the benefits of coffee without the caffeine. Happy brewing!** It said yes without having read anything, and didn't name the blend.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/chat-tools-off" alt="With no tools: the agent says yes without naming the blend" />

The tools also cost tokens. The chat with tools used 3,689 tokens in. The one without used 153.

<Callout type="warn">
A small model doesn't always say when it can't do something. Without its tools, ours guessed instead of saying it couldn't read the file. Check its answers in [Sessions](#read-its-sessions) after you change its tools.
</Callout>

## Change its model

**Model** in **General** lists only the choices that need no key: **AI models in this environment** and **An AI Gateway already in this environment**. To use a provider, first deploy an agent with that provider, which creates a [gateway](/ai/agent-gateway). Then pick the gateway's model here.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/st-model-models" alt="Model on a deployed agent: AI models in this environment (docs-qwen) and An AI Gateway already in this environment (Qwen/Qwen3-4B, ai-gateway)" />

On a gateway model, the hint says **The agent gets its own gateway key, limited to this model: no key to paste.**

We moved our agent from the AI model to the gateway of our second agent, and turned its tools back on in the same change. The commit said **changes model, tools**. After the restart, it answered from its FAQ through the gateway as before.

## Chat with it

**open chat** on the card (or **Open chat** in its menu) opens the team chat page in a new tab, already signed in.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/chat-empty" alt="The team chat page: ai-agent · AI agent, the message box How can I help you today? and suggested prompts" />

- Type in the box and press Enter. The answer appears under the agent's name.
- The model picker in the box lists the agents and AI models of the environment, like **ai-agent · AI agent** and **docs-qwen · Qwen3 4B**. Pick one to talk to it instead.
- The sidebar has **New Chat**, **Search** and your earlier chats.
- Under each answer: edit, copy, read aloud, info, fork the chat, good or bad response, continue and regenerate.
- **Model controls** (the sliders) has **Valves**, **System Prompt** and **Advanced Params**. These are settings of the chat page itself. We put *Answer only in French.* in **System Prompt** and the agent still answered in English, as Bean. To change how the agent behaves, change its instructions.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/chat-model-controls" alt="Model controls open: Controls, Valves, System Prompt and Advanced Params" />

## Read its sessions

**Agent** → **Sessions** lists every chat with the agent: the first message, the channel, the number of turns and how long ago. The menu filters by channel, and **Refresh** reloads the list. Click a chat to read it: its channel, when it started and its tokens, then the messages. A tool the agent used shows as a line, like **used read**.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/p-session-1" alt="Sessions: the chat that used read, with Team chat, 3,689 in / 59 out, used read and the answer" />

**Add to eval** turns the chat's question into a test case: see [Test your agent](/ai/agent-evals#turn-a-real-chat-into-a-test-case). The channels are explained in [Reach your agent](/ai/agent-channels#read-the-sessions).

## Rename it

**Rename** in the card's menu opens **Rename** with **Name**. Click **Rename**: the toast says **Renamed to bean** and the card shows the new name at once, without a commit.

The chat page lists the agent under its new name, for example **Val renamed · AI agent**, once its list refreshes. The agent's service and address keep their names.

## Restart it

**Restart** starts a new instance of the agent at once, with no dialog or commit. Its chats, files and settings stay.

## Pause it

**Pause** stops the agent at once. The card shows **Paused**, and the menu then has **Resume** instead of **Restart** and **Pause**. A paused agent doesn't answer. A message sent to it on the chat page gets: "This agent is paused, so it cannot answer. To resume it, open its project in the Stackblaze dashboard and choose Resume in the agent card’s menu."

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/lc-paused-card" alt="The agent's card: bean, open chat, Paused" />

**Resume** starts it again. Ours showed **Stopped** and then **Running** about 20 seconds later.

## Delete it

<Steps>

<Step>

### Stage the delete

Right-click the card and choose **Delete**. The card says **removes on commit**. The agent's own PostgreSQL is staged for deletion with it.

</Step>

<Step>

### Commit

Click **Commit 2 changes**. The dialog lists the agent under **Services** and its database under **Add-ons**, and warns: **bean and ai-agent-5m73-db are deleted permanently, with any data they hold.** Type the phrase it asks for, here `sudo delete bean ai-agent-5m73-db`, and click **Delete 2 resources**.

<Screenshot id="ai-agent/lc-del1-delete-commit" alt="2 changes to apply: bean (delete) and ai-agent-5m73-db (delete), with the confirmation to type and Delete 2 resources" />

</Step>

</Steps>

What goes with the agent:

- Its chats, knowledge files, skills, keys and webhooks.
- An agent on an AI model, or on a gateway it didn't create, takes its own PostgreSQL with it.
- An agent deployed with its own gateway has no database of its own. We deleted such an agent while another agent used its gateway: the commit listed only the agent (**Delete 1 resource**), and the gateway and its PostgreSQL kept running for the other one. Delete the gateway on its own once no agent uses it: see [AI Gateway](/ai/agent-gateway#delete-it).
- The AI model it used stays. Delete it from its own panel: see [AI models](/ai/models).

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No reply within 120 s.** in the chat | The model took too long. Ours was an AI model that had gone to sleep, and waking it took longer than two minutes. | Ask again once the model is awake. Its card shows its status. |
| The card says **Stopped** right after **Deploy** | The agent is still starting. | Wait about a minute for **Running**. |
| **Optimizations need the agent on its AI Gateway: costs are priced there.** | The agent talks to an AI model directly. | Move it to a gateway model in **General**. |
| **The agent needs 10 completed sessions; it has 5.** | Optimizations build their test from recent chats. | Use the agent more first. |
| **Missing or invalid webhook secret.** (`401`) | The webhook was called with a wrong secret. | Use the secret shown when it was created. |
| A **Deploy** error in **New AI agent** | A field is missing or wrong. | See [Create an agent](/ai/agents#pick-the-model). |
