---
title: Ask Agent
description: Ask the StackBlaze agent about your projects and apps from any page, attach an app with @, and pick up past conversations.
---

**Ask Agent**, in the header on every page, opens the assistant in a side panel. It can look at your projects and apps to answer questions about them. Click the button again, or the panel's close button, to hide it.

## Ask a question

<Steps>

<Step>

### Start a conversation

Click **New conversation** at the top of the panel. Type in **Ask anything…**.

<Screenshot id="ask-agent/3-new" alt="The Ask Agent panel with a new, empty conversation" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Attach a project or app with @

Type `@` to attach context. The list, **ATTACH CONTEXT**, shows your projects and, under each, its apps and add-ons with their environment. Pick one, or keep typing to narrow it down.

<Screenshot id="ask-agent/4-mention" alt="Typing @ lists projects, apps and add-ons to attach" />

Here the question is "How many instances is @nginx-w29b running? Answer in one sentence."

<Screenshot id="ask-agent/5-typed" alt="The question typed with @nginx-w29b attached" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Send it

Press **Enter** or click **Send message**. The panel shows each step the agent takes, then its answer. Ours ran a **Get app** step and answered: "nginx-w29b is running 1 instance (web replicas = 1, with 1 pod ready and 0 restarts)." The attached app is listed under **ATTACHED CONTEXT**.

<Screenshot id="ask-agent/6-answer" alt="The agent's answer: nginx-w29b is running 1 instance, with the Get app step and the attached context" />

</Step>

</Steps>

Next to the message box, **Attach image** adds a picture ("or paste / drop"). Messages you sent have an **Edit message** button.
