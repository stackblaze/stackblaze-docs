<Callout type="info">
This page shows the dialog with `docs-demo.example.com`, a hostname we have no DNS for. It shows what StackBlaze asks for, but the domain never verifies here, so the steps after DNS (verification and the HTTPS certificate) are not shown.

## Step 1: the hostname

Click **+ Custom Domain**. **Add Custom Domain** (**Step 1 of 2**) asks for one hostname, such as `app.example.com`. The line next to the buttons tells you whether it can be added:

| What you type | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing | "Enter a hostname", and **Add Domain** is greyed out |
| Not a hostname (`not a hostname`, `localhost`) | "Enter a valid custom hostname" |
| A `stackblaze.app` hostname | "Enter a valid custom hostname" |
| A hostname that is free | **Available** |

<Screenshot id="settings/domain-add-invalid" alt="Add Custom Domain: Enter a valid custom hostname" />

A pasted URL works too: `https://DOCS-DEMO.EXAMPLE.COM/` was read as `docs-demo.example.com` and shown as **Available**.

<Screenshot id="settings/domain-add-available" alt="Add Custom Domain: docs-demo.example.com, Available" />

Click **Add Domain**.

## Step 2: the DNS records

**Configure DNS Records** (**Step 2 of 2**) says "Add both records at **example.com** so this hostname can resolve and verify." Each value has a copy button.

| | Type | Name | Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **CNAME** | `docs-demo` | a hostname under `edge.stackblaze.cloud`, unique to the service |
| 2 | **TXT** | `_stackblaze-verify.docs-demo` | `stackblaze-verify=` and a token |

The CNAME starts as **Pending** and the TXT as **Required**. Below them: "Waiting for DNS. Records are re-checked every few minutes." and "Not verified yet. Until it is, other accounts can claim this domain too: the first claim whose TXT record verifies gets it, and the other claims are released."

<Screenshot id="settings/domain-dns" alt="Configure DNS Records: CNAME docs-demo Pending, TXT _stackblaze-verify.docs-demo Required" />

**Verify now** checks right away. With no records in place, the dialog and a notification said: "TXT _stackblaze-verify.docs-demo.example.com not found; CNAME docs-demo.example.com → … not found".

<Screenshot id="settings/domain-verify" alt="After Verify now: TXT not found; CNAME not found" />

Click **Done**. The domain is listed under the default URL with **Configure DNS**, which opens the same dialog again, and a **−** button. The canvas toolbar now shows **Commit 1 change**.

<Screenshot id="settings/domain-listed" alt="Custom domains with docs-demo.example.com and Configure DNS" />

## Remove a domain

Click **−** next to the domain. It disappears from the list, and the staged change goes with it.

## After the records are in place

StackBlaze re-checks waiting domains every few minutes. **Verify now** checks one right away; you can use it once every 10 seconds per domain. A domain goes through these states, and the dialog says which one it is in:

| State | What the dialog says |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting for DNS | "Waiting for DNS. Records are re-checked every few minutes." or what is still missing |
| DNS verified | "DNS verified." |
| Certificate being issued | "Verified — issuing the HTTPS certificate (usually a few minutes)." |
| Live | "Verified — HTTPS certificate issued. This domain is live." |

The certificate is issued and renewed for you. You can't upload your own.

### Check the records yourself

```sh
dig +short CNAME docs-demo.example.com
dig +short TXT _stackblaze-verify.docs-demo.example.com
```

The first must print the hostname under `edge.stackblaze.cloud` from the dialog, the second the `stackblaze-verify=` value.

## When verification doesn't go through

| What the dialog or **Verify now** says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "TXT _stackblaze-verify.… not found" | Add the TXT record with the exact name and value. Copy them with the copy buttons. |
| "CNAME … → … not found" | Add the CNAME. If your DNS provider can proxy traffic through its own servers, turn that off for this record, so it answers with the CNAME. |
| "… points to …, expected …" | The CNAME points somewhere else, for example to the service's `stackblaze.app` URL. Change it to the `edge.stackblaze.cloud` hostname from the dialog. |

After you change a record, the old answer can stay in DNS caches until its time-to-live runs out. Then click **Verify now** again.

## Root domains

A root domain such as `example.com` can't have a CNAME record. If your DNS provider offers CNAME flattening, `ALIAS` or `ANAME`, point the root domain at the same `edge.stackblaze.cloud` hostname with it. StackBlaze accepts the domain when it resolves to the same addresses as that hostname. The TXT record is still `_stackblaze-verify` followed by the domain.

## Limits

- **One app per domain.** Once a domain is verified for one app, other accounts can't add it: "… is already in use: another account has verified it. A domain can only be attached to one app once its DNS is verified."
- **20 new domains a day.** One workspace can add at most 20 new hostnames in any 24 hours. The 21st is refused: "This workspace added 20 custom domains in the last 24 hours. Try again later."
- **7 days to verify.** A domain that hasn't verified 7 days after it was added is removed, and the hostname is free again for anyone.
- No redirects. The Domains screen has no setting to send `www` to the root domain or the other way round. Every domain you add serves the app. If you want one address, redirect in your app (see [Redirects](/networking#redirects)).

## Take over a domain

If someone verified your domain on another account and no longer controls its DNS, add it from the CLI with `--takeover`:

```sh
stackblaze domain add docs-demo.example.com --takeover
```

StackBlaze then reports: "Takeover requested: another account has verified this domain. It moves to this app once your TXT and CNAME records are live and the other account's TXT record has been gone for 2 checks at least 10 minutes apart."

## From the CLI

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `stackblaze domain add <host>` | Add the domain and print the TXT and CNAME records |
| `stackblaze domain add <host> --wait` | Also keep checking until it is live (`--timeout`, default 600 s; `--interval`, default 10 s) |
| `stackblaze domain verify <host>` | Check DNS now and move the domain on |
| `stackblaze domain list` | The service's domains with their verification and certificate status |
| `stackblaze domain status [host]` | The service's hostnames and HTTPS |
| `stackblaze domain remove <host>` | Remove the domain and its certificate |

See [`stackblaze domain`](/cli/reference/domain).

## HTTPS in your app

HTTPS ends at the [edge](/networking/edge-network), for custom domains and for the default URL. Your service gets plain HTTP on its port, so don't set up certificates or HTTPS in the app. Listen on [`PORT`](/configure/environment-variables#variables-stackblaze-sets) on `0.0.0.0`. To see the certificate a visitor gets:

```sh
curl -vI https://docs-demo.example.com 2>&1 | grep -iE "subject|issuer|expire"
```
