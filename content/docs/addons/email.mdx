---
title: Email (SMTP)
description: Send email from your apps with no setup — connected services get SMTP_HOST, SMTP_USER and the rest.
---

The **Email (SMTP)** add-on gives your apps an SMTP relay. Connect it to a service and the service gets the `SMTP_*` variables it needs. Sending is metered per email toward your plan's included usage.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Add it

On the canvas, click **+ Service** → **Add-on** (**Kafka, email, and more**) → **Email (SMTP)**.

<Screenshot id="addons/1-menu" alt="The Add-ons menu: Kafka, Memcached, Object storage, ProxySQL, RabbitMQ, Email (SMTP), App Shield and Scheduled tasks" />

An **Email (SMTP)** card appears, marked **Ready to deploy**. Click it: the panel says it "creates it on Dev right away" with the catalog defaults. Click **Deploy** (or **Remove from canvas** to drop it).

<Screenshot id="addons/email-2-review" alt="Email (SMTP): Ready to deploy, with Remove from canvas and Deploy" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Set the sender

When the card shows **Running**, click it. **Overview** shows **Sending** and the **From** address: a **Name** and an **Address** on your project's mail domain (`<project>.dev-mail.stackblaze.cloud` here). The domains on offer come from the custom domains on this project's apps.

<Screenshot id="addons/email-4-sheet" alt="Email (SMTP) Overview: Sending, From Name and Address" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Send a test email

Open **Send a test email**, enter an address and click **Send**. The result shows whether the relay accepted it, for example "Accepted by the relay for … · 1.2 s · 250 Ok". This proves the relay takes the message; check the inbox to see where it lands.

<Screenshot id="addons/email-7-sent" alt="Send a test email: Accepted by the relay, 250 Ok" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Connect a service

Open **Connected apps** and turn on the switch next to the service, then commit and deploy the change (**nginx ⇄ email off → on**).

<Screenshot id="addons/email-9-connections" alt="Connected apps: 0 connected, with a switch for nginx" />

The service gets these variables:

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| `SMTP_HOST` | The relay's address |
| `SMTP_PORT` | `587` (STARTTLS) |
| `SMTP_USER` | The relay user |
| `SMTP_PASSWORD` | The relay password |
| `SMTP_FROM` | The From address, e.g. `noreply@<project>.dev-mail.stackblaze.cloud` |

</Step>

</Steps>

## Credentials for other tools

**Credentials** on **Overview** shows the same five values, each with a copy button; the password is masked. Use them for tools that don't read your app's variables.

<Screenshot id="addons/email-5-credentials" alt="Credentials: SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT 587 (STARTTLS), SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASSWORD and SMTP_FROM" />

## Use your own SMTP provider

The **Provider** tab chooses between **StackBlaze email** (no setup, metered) and **Your own SMTP provider** (not metered; bounces and limits are your provider's). Changing the provider rewrites the `SMTP_*` variables and restarts connected services.

<Screenshot id="addons/email-8-provider" alt="Provider: StackBlaze email or Your own SMTP provider" />

## Delete

**Delete add-on** is at the bottom of **Connected apps**. Disconnect all apps first.
