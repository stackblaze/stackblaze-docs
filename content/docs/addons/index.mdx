---
title: Add-ons
description: Email, scheduled tasks, caching, database routing and event streaming, added to an environment from the canvas.
---

Add-ons run next to your services in an environment. Add one with **+ Service** → **Add-on**, then connect it to the services that use it.

<SectionCards>
  <SectionCard icon="Mail" title="Email (SMTP)" href="/addons/email" description="Send email from your apps with no setup — connected services get SMTP_HOST, SMTP_USER and the rest." />
  <SectionCard icon="CalendarClock" title="Scheduled tasks" href="/addons/scheduled-tasks" description="Run a command on a cron schedule, on its own or inside one of your services' image and variables." />
  <SectionCard icon="MemoryStick" title="Memcached" href="/addons/memcached" description="Add an in-memory cache to an environment and connect it to your services." />
  <SectionCard icon="Split" title="ProxySQL" href="/addons/proxysql" description="Put ProxySQL in front of a MariaDB database for connection pooling and read/write routing, connect an app to it, and see its backends and query rules." />
  <SectionCard icon="Layers" title="Kafka" href="/addons/kafka" description="Add a Kafka event-streaming cluster to an environment, connect your services to it, produce and consume messages, and scale it to three brokers." />
</SectionCards>
