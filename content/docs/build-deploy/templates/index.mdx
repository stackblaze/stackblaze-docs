---
title: Templates
description: One-click apps from the StackBlaze catalog. A template deploys an app together with the databases and add-ons it needs.
---

A template is a ready-made app. When you pick one, StackBlaze adds every service it needs to your environment and deploys them straight away. For example, **uptime-kuma** adds the app, a MariaDB database and an Email add-on.

To deploy one, see [Deploy a template](/build-deploy/templates/deploy).
