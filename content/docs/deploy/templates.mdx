---
title: Deploy a template
description: Deploy a ready-made app from the template catalog. A template can add several services at once, such as an app and its database.
---

Templates are ready-made apps from the StackBlaze catalog. Picking one deploys it right away, with every service it needs. This guide uses **uptime-kuma**, which adds the app and a MariaDB database.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the add menu

In your project, click **+ Service** in the canvas toolbar (or press **Ctrl+K**). Pick **Template** ("One-click apps from the catalog").

<Screenshot id="templates/1-add-menu" alt="The add menu with Template at the top" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Find the template

The **Templates** list opens. Scroll it, or type in **Search templates**. The **All** menu next to the search box filters the list.

<Screenshot id="templates/2-list" alt="The Templates list" />

<Screenshot id="templates/3-search" alt="Searching for uptime: uptime-kuma, Self-hosted uptime monitoring" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick it

Click the template. There is no extra confirmation: StackBlaze deploys it at once. Every service the template needs appears on the canvas, marked **Deploying**. For uptime-kuma that is the **uptime-kuma** app and a **MariaDB** database.

<Screenshot id="templates/4-deploying" alt="uptime-kuma and MariaDB on the canvas, Deploying" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Wait for Running

When every service shows **Running**, the app card shows its public URL.

<Screenshot id="templates/5-running" alt="uptime-kuma and MariaDB, Running, with the app's URL" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Open the app

Click the URL on the app's card.

<Screenshot id="templates/6-app" alt="Uptime Kuma, open at the app's URL" />

</Step>

</Steps>

The services a template adds are ordinary services: open them on the canvas to change their [variables](/configure/environment-variables) and [settings](/configure/service-settings).
