## Create an account

Go to the dashboard's **Create your account** page (**Sign up** on the sign-in page). Sign up with **Continue with GitHub**, **Continue with Google**, or your email and **Continue with email**.

<Screenshot id="signin/4-signup" alt="Create your account: Continue with GitHub, Continue with Google, or an email address and Continue with email" />

## Sign in

**Welcome back** offers every way in:

- **Continue with GitHub** or **Continue with Google**;
- **Sign in with a passkey**, if you added one (see [Account security](/security/account-security#passkeys));
- your email, then **Continue with email**;
- **Sign in with SSO**, for organizations that set up single sign-on.

<Screenshot id="signin/1-login" alt="Welcome back: GitHub, Google, passkey, email and SSO" />

With email, the next step asks for your **Password** and **Sign in**. **Email me a code instead** sends a 6-digit code to your inbox to type in instead of a password. **Go back** returns to the first step.

<Screenshot id="signin/2-password" alt="The password step: Email, Password, Sign in, Forgot password, Email me a code instead and Go back" />

If you turned on two-factor authentication, the **Two-factor authentication** step comes next, after the password or the emailed code alike. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and click **Verify & sign in**. A wrong code shows "Incorrect code. Try again." If you have a passkey too, you can use it here instead.

Lost your phone? Click **Lost your phone? Use a recovery code** and type one of your recovery codes (they look like `xxxxx-xxxxx`). Each code works once; a used or wrong one shows "That recovery code didn't work. Each code works once." **Use your authenticator app** switches back. See [Account security](/security/account-security#two-factor-authentication).

## Forgot your password

**Forgot password?** opens **Reset your password**: enter your account email and click **Send reset link**. The email has a link to set a new password.
