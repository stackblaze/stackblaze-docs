Every service you run on StackBlaze is watched by Coroot, with nothing to install. Coroot sees each service's requests, errors, latency, CPU, memory and logs, works out which services call which, and opens an incident when a service stops meeting its targets.

You open Coroot from the dashboard. It opens in a new tab, already signed in, and shows only the projects of your organization that you can see. It's read-only, with one exception: you can add your own alerting rules.

<Screenshot id="coroot/app-slo" alt="Coroot showing a service's SLO tab: Availability OK, Latency no data, its clients and the latency and errors heatmap" />

## Where Coroot shows up in the dashboard

- **Open monitoring**, on a service's **Metrics** tab, opens that service in Coroot.
- **Observability**, in the sidebar of the projects list, opens Coroot for all your projects.
- When a service has an open incident, the dashboard flags it: an **Issue** badge on the project, a banner above the canvas with **View in monitoring**, and an **Incident** badge on the service's card. See [Incidents](/coroot/incidents).
- The charts on a service's [Metrics](/observe/metrics) tab come from the same monitoring.
