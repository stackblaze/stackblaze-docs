title: Valkey
description: Add Valkey (a Redis-compatible key/value store) to an environment, connect an app, and manage its storage and high availability.
---

Valkey is a fast in-memory key/value store, compatible with Redis. Like every database, it belongs to one environment, and any app in that environment can connect to it.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Add Valkey

On the project canvas, click **+ Service**, then **Database or cache**, and pick **Valkey**. A card appears under **Databases** marked **Ready to deploy**.

<Screenshot id="valkey/1-ready" alt="The Valkey card on the canvas, Ready to deploy" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Deploy it

Click the card. It deploys "with the catalog defaults"; size, HA and connections are tuned in its panel once it is running. Click **Deploy** (or **Remove from canvas** to drop it).

<Screenshot id="valkey/2-review" alt="Valkey review: Ready to deploy, with Remove from canvas and Deploy" />

When it is ready, the card shows **Running**.

<Screenshot id="valkey/3-running" alt="The Valkey card, Running" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Connect an app

Click **Valkey** on the card, open **Settings**, and in **Connections** turn on the switch next to your app. Then **Commit 1 change** and **Deploy 1 change**. The app redeploys.

<Screenshot id="valkey/6-connect" alt="Connections with nginx switched on, marked staged" />

Connecting added these variables to the app:

| Variable | |
|---|---|
| `REDISHOST`, `REDISPORT` | the in-cluster address and port (`6379`) |
| `REDIS_HOST`, `REDIS_PORT` | the same address and port, under the names the **Environment snippet** uses |
| `REDIS_URL` | the same as a `redis://` URL |

<Screenshot id="valkey/7-variables" alt="The app's variables with REDISHOST, REDISPORT and REDIS_URL" />

From the connected app, `redis-cli -u "$REDIS_URL"` reached the database:

```text
PING            → PONG
SET docs hello EX 60   → OK
GET docs        → "hello"
```

</Step>

</Steps>


## Connection details

**Settings** → **Access** → **Connect from apps** shows the **Host**, the short **Service** name, the **Password** and the **Database index** (`0`). On our database the password was **(none)**: the database is only reachable from apps in the same environment ("not reachable from the public internet"). **Connection URL** and **Environment snippet** give the same details ready to paste.

<Screenshot id="valkey/5-access" alt="Access: Host, Service, Password (none), Database index 0, Connection URL and Environment snippet" />

Valkey has no **Public access** setting.

## Plan limits

The top of **Settings** shows what your plan allows: **Redis memory** (how much data it can hold; "when it's full, the oldest keys are dropped") and **Redis connections** (how many clients can connect at once).

<Screenshot id="valkey/4-limits" alt="Plan limits: Redis memory and Redis connections" />

## Storage

**Settings** → **Storage** shows the volume and **Expand storage**. Volumes can only grow. We expanded ours from 1 GiB to 10 GiB; the sizes offered were 10, 20, 30, 40 and 100 Gi.

<Screenshot id="valkey/11-storage" alt="Storage: the volume and Expand storage" />

<Screenshot id="valkey/12-storage-expanded" alt="Storage after expanding: 10 GiB" />

## High availability

A new Valkey runs as a **Single primary**. **Settings** → **HA** → **Enable HA** asks first: it "Adds a replica (master + 1 replica). This is a one-way upgrade — there is no in-place downgrade to a solo master."

<Screenshot id="valkey/13-ha-off" alt="High availability: Single primary, with Enable HA" />

<Screenshot id="valkey/14-ha-confirm" alt="Enable high availability? Adds a replica" />

Ours came up **Healthy** with **2/2** ready: one primary and one replica.

<Screenshot id="valkey/15-ha-on" alt="High availability: Master + 1 replica, Healthy, 2/2 ready" />

## Deployments, logs and metrics

The panel's other tabs work like PostgreSQL's (see [PostgreSQL](/data/databases/postgresql#deployments)): **Deployment** lists deploys and restarts, **Logs** streams the database's output.

<Screenshot id="valkey/8-deployment" alt="Valkey: the Deployment tab" />

<Screenshot id="valkey/9-logs" alt="Valkey: the Logs tab" />

**Metrics** shows the last **2h**, **24h** or **7d**, with **Refresh**. At the top: **Clients** (connected), **Cache hit** (keyspace hits), **Memory** (dataset + overhead) and **Ops** (commands per second). Below that are charts:

| Group | Charts |
|---|---|
| Host resources | Memory usage, CPU usage |
| Workload | Connected clients, Operations / s |
| Health | Keyspace hit ratio |
| Storage | Used memory, Keys, Evicted keys / s, Expired keys / s, Blocked clients |

<Screenshot id="valkey/10-metrics" alt="Valkey: the Metrics tab" />

## Delete

**Delete add-on** at the bottom of **Settings** deletes the database and its data. Disconnect its apps first.
