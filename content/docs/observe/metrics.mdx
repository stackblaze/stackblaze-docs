Open a service on the canvas and click **Metrics**.

<Screenshot id="metrics/1-tab" alt="The Metrics tab: Avg CPU, Avg Memory, Avg Resp. Time and Avg Throughput, then the Resources and HTTP charts" />

## Pick the window

At the top, choose **2h**, **24h** or **7d**. Every number and chart follows that window. **Refresh** reloads them.

The four cards give the average over the window: **Avg CPU**, **Avg Memory**, **Avg Resp. Time** and **Avg Throughput**.

## The charts

Each chart shows its average and maximum over the window.

**Resources** ("CPU and memory over the selected window"):

- **Memory usage**, the memory the service uses;
- **CPU usage**, in mCPU (thousandths of a CPU).

**HTTP** ("Request rate and latency"):

- **Response time**;
- **Throughput**, in requests per second;
- **HTTP status codes**, the share of requests answered with a 4xx or 5xx error, with the split by class (for example **2xx 100%**);
- **Response traffic**, the data sent back to visitors, in KB/s.

On **24h** the time axis runs across the day; on **7d** it shows dates.

<Screenshot id="metrics/range-7d" alt="The Metrics tab on 7d, with dates on the time axis" />

## Check performance and monitoring

- **Check performance** asks [Ask Agent](/ai/ask-agent) why the service is slow ("Why is @nginx-w29b slow?"). The agent's answer appears in the Ask Agent panel.
- **Open monitoring** opens the full monitoring view for the service.
