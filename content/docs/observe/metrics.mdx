---
title: Metrics
description: CPU, memory, response time and throughput for a service.
---

Open a service and click **Metrics**.

<Screenshot id="metrics/1-app-metrics" alt="The Metrics tab: average CPU, memory, response time and throughput, with charts" />

- Pick the time window at the top: **2h**, **24h** or **7d**.
- The cards show **Avg CPU**, **Avg Memory**, **Avg Resp. Time** and **Avg Throughput** for that window.
- Below them, **Memory usage** and **CPU usage** charts show the average and the maximum.
- **Refresh** reloads the numbers.
