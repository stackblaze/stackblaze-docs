---
title: Notifications
description: Send project events, such as an app being deployed, restarted or updated, to a webhook or a chat channel.
---

A **notification channel** sends the events you pick, for the projects you pick, to one destination. Channels belong to the organization. Open **Settings** → **Notifications**.

<Screenshot id="notifications/1-empty" alt="Notifications: Channels, Create notification, and No notifications yet" />

## Create a channel

<Steps>

<Step>

### Name it and pick the destination

Click **Create notification** and give it a **Name**. Choose the **Type**: **Webhook** for any HTTP endpoint, or one of the two chat services in the menu. **Enabled** is on by default.

For **Webhook**, enter the **Webhook URL** and, if you like, a **Secret**. For a chat service, the dialog asks for that service's webhook URL and a **Channel**.

<Screenshot id="notifications/2-dialog" alt="Create notification: Name, Type, Enabled, the destination fields, Projects and Events" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick projects and events

Under **Projects**, choose **All projects** or the projects you want. Under **Events**, pick the ones that send a notification:

| Projects | Apps | Other |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project created, Project updated, Project deleted | App created, App updated, App deleted, App restarted, App rebuilt, App deployed | Git push received, Settings updated, Backup failed |

Here, one project and two events: **App restarted** and **App updated**.

<Screenshot id="notifications/3-filled" alt="Create notification filled in: docs-webhook, Webhook, one project, App restarted and App updated" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Create it

Click **Create**. The channel is listed with its type and what it covers, here **1 project · 2 events**.

<Screenshot id="notifications/4-listed" alt="The channel docs-webhook: webhook, 1 project · 2 events" />

</Step>

</Steps>

We restarted an app in that project. Within seconds, our webhook URL received a `POST` request for the restart.

## Change or remove a channel

Each channel's row has a switch to turn it off without deleting it, and **Show details**. Its **⋯** menu has **Edit notification** and **Delete notification**.

<Screenshot id="notifications/5-menu" alt="A channel's menu: Edit notification and Delete notification" />

**Delete notification** asks first ("Are you sure you want to delete the notification "docs-webhook"?") and then removes the channel.
