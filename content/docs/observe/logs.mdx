---
title: Logs
description: Follow a service's logs live, filter them by text or instance, switch to system events, and copy or download them.
---

Open a service on the canvas and click **Logs**. New lines appear as the service writes them.

<Screenshot id="logs/1-tab" alt="The Logs tab: App logs, Search logs, Live tail and nginx access log lines" />

Each line shows the time, the instance it came from in brackets (for example `[4gwkq]`), and the text.

## Filter the lines

- **Search logs** keeps only the lines that contain your text. Here, `kube-probe` keeps the health check requests.
- Click an instance tag such as `[4gwkq]` to show only that instance's lines. The search box then shows **instance: 4gwkq**.

<Screenshot id="logs/2-search" alt="Logs filtered by kube-probe: only the health check requests" />

## App logs and system events

The source menu, **App logs** by default, switches to **System events**: what the platform did with the service, such as a container image pulled, a container created and started, or a new version scaled up.

<Screenshot id="logs/4-system-events" alt="System events: Pulled, Created, SuccessfulCreate, ScalingReplicaSet, Scheduled and Started" />

## Live tail and time ranges

**Live tail** follows new lines. Its menu also lists **Last 15 minutes**, **Last 1 hour**, **Last 6 hours**, **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days** and **Last 30 days**. How far back logs are kept depends on your plan; see [Log retention](/observe/tracing-and-log-retention#log-retention).

<Screenshot id="logs/menu-range" alt="The time menu: Live tail, Last 15 minutes up to Last 30 days" />

## Copy and download

The **⋯** menu has **Refresh**, **Copy logs** and **Download logs**.

<Screenshot id="logs/menu-more" alt="The ⋯ menu: Refresh, Copy logs and Download logs" />

## From the CLI

`stackblaze logs` prints the same lines in your terminal, and `-f` follows them live. See [logs](/cli/reference/logs).
