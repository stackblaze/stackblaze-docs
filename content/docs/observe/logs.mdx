---
title: Logs
description: Read and search a service's logs, live.
---

Open a service and click **Logs**.

<Screenshot id="logs/1-app-logs" alt="The Logs tab streaming nginx access logs" />

- Each line shows the time, the instance it came from and the log text.
- **Search logs** filters the lines.
- **Live tail** keeps new lines coming in as the service writes them.
