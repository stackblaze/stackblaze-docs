---
title: Log drains
description: Forward your apps' logs to your own logging service over HTTP or syslog.
---

A log drain sends the logs of the apps you pick to a destination of your own, alongside **Logs** in the dashboard. Drains belong to the organization: one drain can cover apps from several projects and environments.

## Open Log drains

Open **Settings**, then **Log drains** under **Organization**. The page lists your drains, with a search box (**Search drains…**).

<Screenshot id="log-drains/1-empty" alt="Log drains: Drains, Add drain, and No log drains yet" />

## Add a drain

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the form

Click **Add drain**. **Add log drain** opens: "Choose which apps to forward and where their logs go."

<Screenshot id="log-drains/2-dialog" alt="Add log drain: Name, the list of apps, Minimum level, Destination, Endpoint and API key secret" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Name it and pick the apps

- **Name**: how the drain is listed, for example `receiver-logs`.
- **Apps**: every app in the organization, as `app · project / environment`. Type in the box to filter the list, and tick the apps to forward. The line under the list counts them: "1 selected · captures web, worker, build & add-on logs".

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose the level and the destination

- **Minimum level**: **All levels**, **Warn and above** or **Errors only**.
- **Destination**: **HTTP**, **Syslog**, or one of two hosted log services, listed by name.
- **Endpoint**: where the logs go. For **HTTP**, a URL such as `https://logs.example.com/ingest`. For **Syslog**, a host and port such as `syslog.example.com:514`.
- **API key secret** (optional): the name of the secret that holds your destination's API key.

<Screenshot id="log-drains/3-filled" alt="Add log drain filled in: receiver-logs, one nginx app ticked, All levels, HTTP and an endpoint URL" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Save

Click **Save drain**. The drain is listed with its destination type, how many apps it covers and its level, for example **receiver-logs** · **http** · **1 app · Level all**.

<Screenshot id="log-drains/4-listed" alt="The drain listed: receiver-logs, http, 1 app, Level all" />

The endpoint must be a public address that resolves. An endpoint whose host doesn't resolve is refused, for example "logDrains[0].sink.endpoint must be a public destination (host logs.example.com does not resolve)".

</Step>

</Steps>

## Edit or delete a drain

The **⋯** button on a drain has **Edit drain** and **Delete drain**.

<Screenshot id="log-drains/5-menu" alt="The drain's menu: Edit drain and Delete drain" />

**Edit drain** opens the same form as **Edit log drain**. We ticked a second app and clicked **Save drain**; the drain then read **2 apps**.

<Screenshot id="log-drains/5-edit" alt="Edit log drain: 2 selected, with the same level, destination and endpoint" />

**Delete drain** asks to confirm: "This removes the drain from all selected apps." Click **Remove**. The drain is gone from the list and from every app it covered.

<Screenshot id="log-drains/6-remove" alt="Remove drain receiver-logs? This removes the drain from all selected apps. Cancel, Remove" />

## From the CLI

`stackblaze log-drains` manages the same drains. Every option is in the [log-drains reference](/cli/reference/log-drains).
