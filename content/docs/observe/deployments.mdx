---
title: Deployments
description: See every deploy of a service, who made it and what changed.
---

Open a service and click **Deployment**. The list shows events, deploys and rollbacks, newest first.

<Screenshot id="deployments/1-list" alt="The Deployment tab with two deploys" />

Each row shows:

- the image (for example `nginx:latest`) and its status, such as **rolling out** or **running**;
- when it happened, how long it took and who made it;
- the commit message, if one was given when the change was committed (**First Deploy** for the first one).

A deploy that is still rolling out has **Cancel**.

## Deploy again

**Manual Deploy** at the top of the list. For a service that runs a Docker image it offers:

- **Deploy latest reference**: pull the image reference again and deploy it.
- **Restart service**: restart what is running.

<Screenshot id="deployments/2-manual-deploy" alt="The Manual Deploy menu: Deploy latest reference, Restart service" />

## Actions on a past deploy

The **⋯** menu on a deploy row offers **Redeploy**, **Inspect Deployment**, **Copy URL** and **Visit**.

<Screenshot id="deployments/3-row-menu" alt="The row menu: Redeploy, Inspect Deployment, Copy URL, Visit" />
